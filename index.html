Where can I send money?+

To 100+ countries from Hong Kong. Popular corridors include the Philippines, India, Indonesia, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh — see the live rates above.

How much does a transfer cost?+

Competitive rates and the lowest charges, with no hidden fees. Your exact rate and fee are always shown before you pay.

How do I pay for a transfer?+

Pay securely by bank transfer once your recipient's details and amount are set up.

Yes. Wirease is regulated by the Customs and Excise Department (C&ED) of Hong Kong and numerous authorities around the world, with funds safeguarded at leading banks.

Yes — business payments of any size, multi-currency accounts, wholesale banknotes in 50+ currencies, and a white-label IMTS platform for partners. Email business@wirease.com.
